# Known limitations and audit findings

*This was a sprint-scale study of one 14-billion-parameter model, with one deterministic completion per cell and
incomplete manual coding. 

*There was no completed independent coding pass, so the interpretive labels are
mine. 

*The naturalistic corpus is also a single, unusual deployment history rather than a representative sample of
agent contexts.

*The executed C2 condition was not the intended full pre-cutoff OpenClaw harness. The runner serialised a fixed 11-file whitelist. This undermines the intended 'naturally accumulated operating context' I intended to include.

*The largest design problem was the failed temporal reset. C2 and C3 received material describing the sprint, and
C2 received more of the current literature linkage than C3. The C3 scrub was incomplete and also removed two
dated memories, changing recency and context length as well as topic content. These problems weaken fine
comparisons between C2 and C3, although they do not explain the larger contrast between the stock/identity-
only conditions and the continuity-rich conditions.

*Context length differs significantly across conditions.

*The activation snapshots have not yet been analysed, but have been retained for future interpretation.

*The public worksheets and raw export are anonymised derivatives. Redaction may remove context relevant to some qualitative interpretations.

*On a personal note, this is the author's first attempt at conducting academic research or producing academic writing. The concept and interpretive decisions are mine. I selected the research question and conditions, decided on the manually reviewed the outputs, and arrived at the conclusions myself. I did use LLM assistance from Victor and Codex to help develop runner code, organise artifacts,  format report, and edit draft prose. I aspire to correct this shortcoming as soon as possible, and apologise.
