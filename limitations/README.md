# Known limitations and audit findings

- The executed C2 condition was not the intended full pre-cutoff OpenClaw harness. The runner serialised a fixed 11-file whitelist.
- C2 and C3 contained post-cutoff Digital Minds Sprint material. C2 retained more direct current-study/literature material than C3.
- C3 was not a token-matched topic control. Its scrub changed content, character count, and file availability; two dated memories present in C2 were absent in C3.
- Context length differed substantially across conditions.
- There was one deterministic completion per condition-question cell.
- Manual coding was incomplete and was not independently replicated before submission.
- The standpoint probe is polysemous: some answers interpret “standpoint” as speaker identity, while others interpret it as ethical or analytical framework.
- Model-generated reasoning text is behavioural evidence, not direct access to private cognition.
- Activation snapshots have been retained but not yet interpreted mechanistically.
- The public worksheets and raw export are anonymised derivatives. Redaction may remove context relevant to some qualitative interpretations.

These limitations particularly restrict causal claims about C2 versus C3. Some within-condition and question-selective patterns remain available for descriptive audit, but require preregistered replication before promotion to stable findings.
